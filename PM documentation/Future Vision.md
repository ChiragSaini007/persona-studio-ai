# Persona Studio AI Future Vision

The long-term opportunity is to help creators and celebrities turn their public voice into an owned AI engagement layer.

## Possible Product Expansion

1. Text persona

   Fans chat with a creator's AI persona through a public link.

2. Voice persona

   Fans hear replies in a creator-approved voice style, with clear AI disclosure.

3. Video persona

   Fans receive short AI video responses using approved avatar/video tooling.

4. Monetized access

   Creators charge for premium fan chats, priority responses, or subscriber-only personas.

5. Content generation

   Creators use their persona model to draft IG posts, YouTube scripts, shorts, replies, captions, and campaign concepts.

6. Celebrity management

   Teams manage approval, review queues, brand safety, campaigns, and analytics for larger public figures.
