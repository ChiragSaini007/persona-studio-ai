# Persona Studio AI Product Roadmap

This roadmap is intentionally paused for execution until the MVP is top notch.

## Future Vision

Persona Studio AI can evolve from text-based fan chat into a creator/celebrity AI platform for audience monetization, fan engagement, and content generation.

## Future Direction

- Voice fan conversations
- Video fan conversations
- Paid fan access
- Creator subscriptions
- VIP fan tiers
- Team/admin controls for celebrities
- Brand-safe content generation
- Instagram and YouTube content generation
- Avatar/video generation similar to HeyGen-style workflows
- Creator analytics and audience intelligence

## Current Priority

Before building these, the MVP must prove:

- creators can create personas easily
- fans actually engage
- answers feel grounded and natural
- safety/fallback behavior is reliable
- creators trust the dashboard and review controls
