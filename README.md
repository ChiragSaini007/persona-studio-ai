# Persona Studio AI

An AI platform that lets creators publish a branded chat persona so fans can DM and chat with an AI version of them — a creator-operations surface for shaping a public voice, approving its boundaries, and reviewing conversations, not a generic chatbot builder.

## Why I built this
Explored how creators could turn their voice and content into an approvable, on-brand AI persona for fan engagement, while keeping the creator in control of what the AI can and can't say — part of my ongoing work on creator-economy products.

## How it works
Creators go through an interview-style onboarding to shape their persona, then publish a public fan-chat link (`/p/[handle]`). Fan-facing replies are generated by OpenAI, grounded in creator-approved content, with visible AI disclosure and moderation on flagged messages.

## Stack
Next.js (TypeScript) · OpenAI (gpt-5-mini) · Supabase (database + auth) · Stripe (paid conversations)

## Status
Working demo / pre-launch — creator onboarding, persona publishing, and fan chat are built; production hardening (RLS policies, rate limits, audit logs) is still in progress.
